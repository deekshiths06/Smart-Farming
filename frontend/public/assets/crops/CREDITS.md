# Crop image credits

Crop-specific photographs used on the Crop Recommendations page and the crop
detail view. Each file is a locally stored copy of a Wikimedia Commons image
chosen to depict that crop (never packaging, a generic field, or another crop).

Most files are the representative image of the crop's English Wikipedia article
(verified via the Commons API `ImageDescription` and licence metadata). Where the
article's lead image was a botanical illustration, a harvested-produce/market
shot, or otherwise not the growing crop, a crop-specific growing-plant or field
photograph of the same species was chosen from Wikimedia Commons instead.

## Replacing an image

1. Put the new photo in `frontend/public/assets/crops/<crop-id>.jpg`.
2. Update its row above with the subject, author and licence.
3. Keep the crop id matching `CROP_IMAGE_SRC` in `frontend/src/lib/cropImages.ts`.

Crops without an entry fall back to a neutral placeholder labelled with the
crop name (never a misleading photograph).
